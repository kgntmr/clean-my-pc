Quietpane - HOW TO USE
Developed by KomodoWorks.com  |  free  |  open source  |  collects nothing


STEP 1 - UNZIP IT (important)
   Quietpane cannot start from inside the zip file.
   Right-click Quietpane.zip  >  Extract All  >  Extract.
   A new Quietpane folder opens.

STEP 2 - START IT
   In the new folder, double-click "Start Quietpane".
   (You never need to open the "App files" folder.)

   Windows may ask a few questions first. This is normal:
   - "Windows protected your PC"          -> click "More info", then "Run anyway"
   - "Do you want to run this file?"      -> click "Run"
   - "Allow this app to make changes?"    -> click "Yes"

STEP 3 - CLICK ONE BUTTON
   Click the big "Quiet my PC now" button, then "Yes".
   It takes about a minute. Restart your PC when it says "All done!".

   It also quietens the extras your laptop maker left running - MSI, ASUS,
   Dell, HP, Lenovo, Acer - and the Intel, AMD or NVIDIA reporting bits,
   if your PC has any. Your drivers are never touched.


A GOOD START, NOT A GUARANTEE
   Quietpane clears out the usual troublemakers, but it cannot promise a
   PC is clean. If yours still feels wrong afterwards, run a deeper scan
   with a dedicated security tool too.


CHANGED YOUR MIND?
   Click "Undo everything" straight away, or open Quietpane again later
   and use the Undo tab. Files it cleaned are in your Recycle Bin.

JUST WANT A CHECK-UP THAT CHANGES NOTHING?
   Double-click "Safety scan only".

   The check asks Microsoft Defender what it has found and looks around
   for the tricks adware uses. Anything found is sorted into Critical,
   High, Medium, Low or Info, and each item says who found it. Threat
   names come from Defender - Quietpane explains them in plain words.
   You choose what happens next; nothing is removed behind your back.

A WINDOW FULL OF CODE OPENED, OR WINDOWS ASKED "HOW DO YOU WANT TO OPEN THIS FILE?"
   You opened one of the app's own files. Close it (pick nothing) and
   double-click "Start Quietpane" instead.

WHY DOES WINDOWS SAY "UNKNOWN PUBLISHER"?
   Quietpane is free and new, and is not digitally signed yet (we have
   applied for free code signing). Every file is plain text, so anyone can
   read exactly what it does.

WANT TO REMOVE QUIETPANE?
   Removing it does not undo its changes. If you want your PC back
   exactly as it was, click "Undo" first. Then delete the Quietpane
   folder (and any Quietpane-Report files on your Desktop).
   Nothing is installed, so that is all.

   Your undo history stays in C:\ProgramData\Quietpane. If you change
   your mind later, download Quietpane again and use the Undo tab.
   Delete that folder too once you are sure you won't need it.
