CLEAN MY PC - HOW TO USE
Developed by KomodoWorks.com  |  free  |  open source  |  collects nothing


1. UNZIP FIRST
   If you are looking at this inside the zip file:
   right-click CleanMyPC.zip  >  Extract All  >  Extract.

2. START IT
   Double-click "Start Clean My PC".

   Windows may ask a few questions first:
   - "Windows protected your PC"          -> click "More info", then "Run anyway"
   - "Do you want to run this file?"      -> click "Run"
   - "Allow this app to make changes?"    -> click "Yes"

   Why? Clean My PC is free and new, so it is not signed with a paid
   certificate yet. Every file is plain text - you can open them in
   Notepad and read exactly what they do.

3. CLICK ONE BUTTON
   Click the big "Clean my PC now" button.
   It tells you what it will do, you click Yes, and it is done
   in about a minute. Restart your PC when it says "All done!".


CHANGED YOUR MIND?
   Click "Undo everything" straight away, or open Clean My PC again later
   and use the Undo tab. Files it cleaned are in your Recycle Bin.

JUST WANT A CHECK-UP THAT CHANGES NOTHING?
   Double-click "Safety scan only".
